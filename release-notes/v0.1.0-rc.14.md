## 发布状态

- **0.1.0-rc.14** 补齐真实图片读取的权限卡、授权续跑、取消与超时。正式版仍为 0.0.18；本版用于继续评测。

## 图片授权与继续执行

- 需要授权时，主对话自动显示图片权限卡，说明具体文件、交给当前模型及授权范围；回复状态明确为“等待你的授权”。
- “仅这一次”或“本轮允许此图片”会继续刚才的读取，不需要重发消息或重新运行整个任务。本轮许可仍限定当前机器人及其任务、具体图片和 30 分钟，不会一并批准其他操作。
- 可以拒绝或取消本次读取；停止回复也会结束它正在等待的权限请求。等待授权最多 5 分钟，超时后明确说明本次没有读取图片。
- 已取消、停止或超时的卡片关闭旧按钮，重复或迟到的批准不能重新激活旧调用。历史卡片与本地决定保持一致。

## 界面与验证

- 回复进行中的状态文字恢复扫光效果；等待授权时使用静态提示，不误显示为正在查看图片。取消与超时结果在卡片和回复详情中分别表达。
- 更新中英文使用说明、README 与站点更新日志，解释授权后继续、取消和超时流程。
- 源码 Electron 中以真实图片调用、生产权限卡和原生解码验证允许一次、本轮授权、拒绝、取消、停止与超时；模型提出调用使用受控执行器，不代表真实云模型看图质量。

## 升级与限制

- 升级前备份完整数据根，Desktop、CLI/TUI 与 Gateway 同步升级；回退使用完整升级前备份。
- Windows/Linux 交互安装、完整读屏、真实云模型质量与长期日用仍需单独验收。本版不等于正式版验收完成。

## Release status

- **0.1.0-rc.14** completes permission cards, continuation, cancellation and timeouts for actual image reads. Stable remains 0.0.18; this prerelease is for continued evaluation.

## Image approval and continuation

- An image that needs approval automatically displays a card in the main conversation, with the exact file, model use and scope. Reply activity explicitly says it is waiting for approval.
- Allowing once or allowing this image for the round continues the original read without resending a message or restarting the whole task. Round approval remains limited to the current Bot and its tasks, the exact image and 30 minutes; unrelated operations are not approved together.
- Reject or cancel the read directly. Stopping the reply also ends its pending permission requests. Approval waits expire after five minutes and clearly state that the image was not read.
- Cancelled, stopped and expired cards close their old actions. Duplicate or late approval cannot reactivate an ended call, and historical cards reflect the local decision.

## Interface and verification

- Restore the shimmer on active reply status text. Permission waits use a static approval message rather than claiming to be viewing an image. Cards and reply details distinguish cancellation from timeout.
- Update bilingual guidance, READMEs and the site changelog to explain continuation, cancellation and timeouts.
- Source Electron checks use actual image calls, production approval UI and native decoding for allow-once, round approval, rejection, cancellation, stop and timeout. Model intent is controlled in these checks; they do not establish real cloud vision quality.

## Upgrade and limits

- Back up the complete data root and upgrade Desktop, CLI/TUI and Gateway together. Rollback requires the complete pre-upgrade backup.
- Interactive Windows/Linux installation, full screen-reader coverage, real cloud model quality and long-term daily use require separate acceptance. This is not stable-release acceptance.
