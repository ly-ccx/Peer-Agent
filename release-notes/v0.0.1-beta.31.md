## 新增

- **GPT-5.6 模型档位**：订阅模型目录新增 Sol、Terra、Luna 三档，便于按任务复杂度选择合适的推理能力。

## 优化

- **当前问题导航更清晰**：滚动长对话时展示当前回合问题，并与正文内容区保持统一对齐，方便确认上下文。
- **界面毛玻璃效果统一**：当前问题条与「我的消息」导航面板采用一致的半透明模糊效果，提升层级辨识度。
- **流式回答结构更稳定**：优化思考内容与最终正文的分组处理，减少生成过程中内容归类不一致的问题。

## 修复

- 修复当前问题条左右边界不对称的问题。
- 修复连续流式片段中思考区与最终回答可能错误连接的问题。

## What's New

- **GPT-5.6 model tiers**: Subscription catalog adds Sol, Terra, and Luna tiers so you can match reasoning strength to task complexity.

## Improvements

- **Clearer current-question navigation**: Long chats show the current turn’s question aligned with the content column.
- **Unified frosted-glass treatment**: Current-question bar and “My messages” nav share the same translucent blur.
- **More stable stream structure**: Better grouping of thinking vs final body to reduce mis-classification mid-generation.

## Fixes

- Fixed asymmetric left/right padding on the current-question bar.
- Fixed thinking and final answer incorrectly joining across consecutive stream fragments.
