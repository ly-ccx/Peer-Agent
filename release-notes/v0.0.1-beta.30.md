## 优化

- **思考区默认折叠、更清爽**：AI 思考过程默认收起，只显示一行「正在思考」并带光扫动效；需要查看细节时手动展开即可。

## 修复

- 修复思考面板在生成过程中反复展开/收起的抖动问题（活跃态改为整段流式期间稳定判定）。
- 修复需要你选择/回复时，选项面板被折叠区遮住看不见的问题（交互卡现始终显示在折叠区之外）。
- 修复打包后应用启动崩溃的问题：runtime-core 现随包编译产出正式产物，不再把 TypeScript 源码打进安装包导致主进程加载失败。

## Improvements

- **Thinking area defaults to collapsed**: AI thinking stays collapsed to a single “Thinking…” row with a shimmer; expand only when you need detail.

## Fixes

- Fixed thinking panel flapping open/closed during generation (active state stays stable for the whole stream).
- Fixed option panels being hidden under collapsed regions when a reply/choice is required—interaction cards now stay outside the collapse.
- Fixed packaged app launch crash: runtime-core now ships compiled artifacts instead of TypeScript sources that broke main-process loading.
