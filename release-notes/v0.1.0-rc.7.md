## 发布状态

- **0.1.0-rc.7** 是供负责人继续评测产品交互的候选版。正式版仍为 0.0.18；总体验收、连续七天日用和正式版批准仍按原计划执行。

## 交互改进

- 保留原有流式正文。实际工具进行中显示动作、目标、耗时和扫光；点击可查看步骤，再展开参数、返回内容与技术详情。完成、停止或失败后结束动画，保留已发生的事实与展开状态。
- 委派工作显示具体任务名和最新本机状态，可展开当前进展并打开任务；需要用户处理的工作提供直接入口。状态读取失败或记录缺失时说明暂不可用。
- 回复下方的证据收为一个“结果依据”入口，按需查看证据、相关记忆与过程；通知方式移入详情。选择提问继续跟随所属回复，自由输入回答后收起对应选项。
- Bot 概况介绍身份、职责、关联项目、实际配置和正在进行的任务。记忆页先显示已保存内容，来源和管理设置按需展开；过程与证据使用独立查看页面，返回后恢复原档案页签。
- 修复切换思考强度时的宽度和弹层跳动；保存期间保持选择，失败时提示并回退。流式增量到达时保留用户上翻后的阅读位置，完成回复也不会重置已打开的详情。
- 更新中英发布说明、用户文档与站点更新日志；升级矩阵保留 RC6 并加入 RC7。

- 退出旧宿主并备份完整数据根，Desktop、CLI/TUI 与 Gateway 同步升级，无需重置数据。npm 固定版本为 `@peer-agent/cli@0.1.0-rc.7`，`@rc` 跟随后续候选；CLI 归档的 peer 与凭据助手须保持同目录。

## Release status

- **0.1.0-rc.7** is a candidate for continued owner evaluation of product interactions. Stable remains 0.0.18; comprehensive acceptance, seven days of daily use and stable-release approval remain required.

## Interaction improvements

- Retain incremental reply text. Active tools show the actual action, target, elapsed time and scanning highlight. Open steps, then expand parameters, returned content and technical details. Completion, stop or failure ends the animation while preserving facts and expanded state.
- Delegated work shows its specific title and latest local status, with expandable progress and a task entry. Work needing user attention has a direct action. Missing records or failed reads show that status is unavailable.
- Group reply evidence under one result-basis entry with evidence, relevant memories and process on demand. Notification metadata moves into details. Questions remain with their reply and matching choices collapse after a free-form answer.
- Bot Overview describes identity, responsibility, associated project, effective configuration and current work. Memory starts with saved content, with provenance and management settings on demand. Process and evidence have separate detail views that return to the previous profile tab.
- Fix reasoning-effort trigger and popup movement. Keep the pending selection and restore it with an error notice on save failure. New streamed content preserves the reading position after scrolling up; completed replies retain opened details.
- Update bilingual release notes, user documentation and the site changelog. The upgrade matrix retains RC6 and adds RC7.

## Upgrade and evaluation

- RC4–RC6 can check for RC7 through Auto or Beta in Settings → Update & About. Older Beta5/RC1–RC3 require a manual installer matching their architecture from this page.
- Quit older hosts, back up the complete data root and upgrade Desktop, CLI/TUI and Gateway together. No reset is required. Use `@peer-agent/cli@0.1.0-rc.7` for a fixed npm version; `@rc` follows later candidates. Keep peer and its credential helper together in the CLI archive.
- The [RC7 handoff and release checks](https://github.com/ly-ccx/Peer-Knowledge/blob/PeerAgent/pa/rc-live-process/playbook/v0.1.0/rc7-candidate.md) record source, CI, signed artifacts and verification scope. Continue using the [comprehensive test guide](https://github.com/ly-ccx/Peer-Knowledge/blob/PeerKnowledge/pa/rc-05-docs/playbook/v0.1.0/acceptance-test-guide.md) for owner evaluation.

## Validation and known limits

- The fix source passes Mac/Linux build, type, architecture, complete tests and native-window regression checks. Window checks use the production presentation chain with isolated cognition/session fixtures, covering streaming, stop/failure/retry, task status, keyboard, both themes, attachments and effort saves; they do not establish live model quality or real task acceptance.
- Executed-command descriptors for complex writes may remain incomplete in Evidence snapshots, causing independent verification to reject results. This gap still blocks overall readiness and GA.
- Interactive Windows/Linux installation, production remote deployment, physical phones, oversized attachments, complete screen-reader testing and seven-day use require their corresponding environments.
