## 发布说明

- **0.1.0-rc.31** 在 RC30 上收紧回复中的操作与状态表达，并修复引用打开、草稿保存和回复中切换模型的问题。正式版仍为 0.0.18。

## 新功能与优化

- 回复详情和引用只在悬停或键盘焦点时出现，跟气泡正文左右对齐；生成中的回复只显示当前状态。触控和高对比度下这两个动作保持可见。
- 正在读取对话、正在恢复草稿使用同一套弱化状态字，并与输入框所在列对齐。
- macOS 上收起机器人列表后，对话标题右移以避开红绿灯，标题栏高度不变。

## 修复

- 回复进行中不能切换模型，选择菜单保持关闭并说明原因。
- 保存输入草稿不再为整份机器人历史做投影，减少长会话里的保存开销。
- 打不开的回复引用会标明尝试过的项目根；PDF 与 Office 等封装文档改为用系统应用打开，不再当作正文读取。

## 升级与范围

- 升级前备份完整数据目录，Desktop、CLI/TUI 与 Gateway 应使用匹配版本。本版不引入新的数据格式；RC29 的监督记录读写版本限制继续有效，降级仍需升级前的完整备份。
- 未确认的执行结果继续保留为未知。这些修复不证明旧命令已经成功，也不会重放未知的外部影响。
- 发布操作不安装或重启当前应用。隔离回归与受控模型不能代替真实供应商质量、完整硬件输入法和读屏、各平台安装交互或长期使用验证。

## Release notes

- **0.1.0-rc.31** tightens reply actions and status on RC30, and fixes citation opening, draft saving, and model switching during a reply. Stable remains 0.0.18.

## What's new and improvements

- Reply details and quote appear on hover or keyboard focus and align with the bubble text. A reply that is still generating shows only its live status. Touch and forced colors keep both actions visible.
- Reading a conversation and restoring a draft use the same quiet status treatment, aligned with the composer column.
- On macOS, hiding the bot list shifts the conversation title clear of the traffic lights without making the header taller.

## Fixes

- The model picker stays closed while a reply is in progress and explains why it cannot switch.
- Saving an input draft no longer projects the whole bot history, reducing save cost in long conversations.
- A reply citation that cannot be opened names the project root that was tried. PDF and Office documents open in the system app instead of being read as text.

## Upgrade and limits

- Back up the complete data directory and keep Desktop, CLI/TUI and Gateway on matching versions. This release adds no data format; RC29's supervision reader/writer restrictions still apply, and rollback requires the full pre-upgrade backup.
- Unconfirmed execution outcomes remain unknown. These fixes do not certify old commands or replay unknown external effects.
- Publication does not install or restart your app. Isolated regression and controlled cognition do not establish real-provider quality, full hardware IME or screen-reader coverage, all platform installation interactions or long-duration acceptance.
